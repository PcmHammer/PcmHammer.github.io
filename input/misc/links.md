[PCM Hacking . Net](http://pcmhacking.net) -- Where the action is.

[Dimented24x7's initial investigation into the 411 PCM](https://www.thirdgen.org/forums/diy-prom/462548-cook-book-approach-getting.html) -- His account went silent a couple years ago, but this is really where it all started.

[Operating Systems and PCMs](https://www.hotrodders.com/forum/2002-l31-5-7l-out-chevy-express-freshen-up-build-4068650-singlepost50.html) -- Just for reference.

[Find the latest operating system ID and calibration IDs for any GM VIN](https://tis2web.service.gm.com/tis2web/) -- You can put in your own VIN, or you can first do a web search for a year, model name, and the word "VIN" to find a sample VIN.

[Implementing J1850 VPW on a PIC](https://www.cnblogs.com/shangdawei/p/4769620.html) -- If you want to implement from the ground up for another microcontroller, this might be a good reference.

[OBD2 PIDs and conversion information](http://www.totalcardiagnostics.com/support/Knowledgebase/Article/View/104/0/obd2-pids-for-programmers-technical) -- Because it helps with reverse engineering, and the next thing on the to-do list is a data logger.

[VPW protocol details](http://www.fastfieros.com/tech/vpw_communication_protocol.htm) -- This was very helpful in the early days of the project.

[Where you once could get an AllPro USB adapter.](http://obddiag.net/allpro.html) -- For a while it seemed that these were the best hardware to use with PCM Hammer. And then they went out of production.  :(
