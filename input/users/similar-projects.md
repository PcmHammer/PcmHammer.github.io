PCM Hammer supports (a subset of) 1999-2007 GM vehicles... but what if you want to read and write the ECU firmware of other vehicles?

# Subaru
### 2002 to ~2015
Tactrix makes OpenPort OBD2 interfaces and EcuFlash software.

There's a community of users and hackers at RomRaider.com

# Mitsubishi
### Unknown years
Tactrix makes OpenPort OBD2 interfaces and EcuFlash software.

https://www.evoscan.com/

# Saab
See T5Suite, T7Suite, etc at https://txsuite.org/

# BMW
### M52TU and M54 engines (roughly the E46 era, I think?)

That community also seems to be mostly at RomRaider.com
