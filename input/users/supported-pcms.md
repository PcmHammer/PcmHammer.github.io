PCM Hammer supports a few families of General Motors' Powertrain Control Modules.

# P01 and P59

These were used in several General Motors vehicles from 1999-2007 that had V8 engines from the LS family.

# P04

Primarily used in front-wheel drive (FWD) V6 vehicles from approximately 1999 to 2005. It was most commonly paired with the 3.1L, 3.4L, and 3.8L engines.

Chevrolet: Impala, Monte Carlo, Lumina, and Venture.

Pontiac: Grand Prix, Montana, and Bonneville.

Buick: Century, LeSabre, Regal, Rendezvous, and LaCrosse.

Oldsmobile: Alero, Intrigue, and Silhouette

# P05

Used primarily in mid-2000s front-wheel-drive (FWD) vehicles equipped with 3.4L, 3.5L, and 3.8L V6 engines.

2004–2008 Pontiac Grand Prix: 3.8L (L26) and 3.8L Supercharged (L32) engines.

2005 Chevrolet Equinox: 3.4L (LNJ) engine.

2005 Chevrolet Malibu & Malibu Maxx: 3.5L (LX9) engine.

2005 Pontiac G6: 3.5L (LX9) engine.

2006–2009 Buick LaCrosse / Allure: 3.8L (L26) engine.

2006–2008 Buick Lucerne: 3.8L (L26)

# P08

Used to manage 2.2L (L43/LN2) and 2.4L (LD9) naturally aspirated, non-Ecotec four-cylinder engines in late-1990s and early-2000s GM vehicles.

Chevrolet Cavalier (1997–2002)

Pontiac Sunfire (1997–2002)

Chevrolet S10 Pickup (1997–2003, 2.2L)

GMC Sonoma (1997–2003, 2.2L)

Oldsmobile Alero (1999–2004, 2.2L & 2.4L)

Pontiac Grand Am (1997–2001, 2.4L)

Buick Skylark (1997–1998, 2.4L)

Oldsmobile Achieva (1997–1998, 2.4L)

Saturn L-Series (2000–2002, 2.2L)

# P10

Used in a few vehicles equipped with the 4.2L Vortec 4200 (LL8) inline-6 engine, during the 2002 through 2005 model years. It was then replaced by the P12 PCM.

Chevrolet TrailBlazer / TrailBlazer EXT

GMC Envoy / Envoy XL / Envoy XUV

Oldsmobile Bravada

Buick Rainier

Chevrolet Colorado (2004–2005 models)

GMC Canyon (2004–2005 models)

Isuzu Ascender

Chevrolet SSR

# P11

Used with the 2.2L Ecotec (L61) 4-cylinder engine in GM's compact "J-body" cars between 2002 and 2005.

2002–2005 Chevrolet Cavalier

2002–2005 Pontiac Sunfire

2002–2004 Saturn L-Series (with the 2.2L engine)

# P12

Used from 2004 to 2007 to manage specific Atlas inline engines, some front-wheel-drive four-cylinders, and supercharged performance engines, including:

Midsize SUVs (Atlas 4.2L): Chevrolet TrailBlazer, GMC Envoy, Buick Rainier CXL, Isuzu Ascender, Saab 9-7X

Compact Trucks & SUVs (Atlas 2.8L, 2.9L, 3.5L, 3.7L): Chevrolet Colorado, GMC Canyon, Isuzu i-Series, Hummer H3

Sport Coupes & Hatchbacks (2.0L Supercharged): Chevrolet Cobalt SS, Saturn ION Redline, Ariel Atom

Sedans (2.2L): Chevrolet Malibu

# E54

Used in 2001–2004 heavy-duty trucks and medium-duty commercial vehicles equipped with the 6.6L LB7 Duramax Diesel engine.

# E60

Used with the 6.6L LLY Duramax Diesel engine in the 2004.5 to 2006 model years.

Chevrolet Silverado 2500HD & 3500HD

GMC Sierra 2500HD & 3500HDGMC C4500 / C5500 Kodiak (Medium-duty trucks)

Hummer H1 Alpha

# BlackBox

Used in 1996 to 2000 full-size GM trucks and SUVs, with Vortec V8s and the 4.3L V6 engine.

Chevrolet Silverado / GMC Sierra (GMT400 platform): 1996–2000 (equipped with 4.3L V6, 5.0L V8, 5.7L V8, or 7.4L V8 engines).

Chevrolet Tahoe / GMC Yukon: 1996–2000

Chevrolet Suburban / GMC Suburban: 1996–2000

Chevrolet Full-Size Vans (Express/G-Series): 1996–2000

GMC Savana Vans: 1996–2000

Chevrolet Astro / GMC Safari: 1996–2000 (often equipped with the 4.3L V6)
